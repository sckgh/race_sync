A field of remote sim racers races your real SuperTT enduro — the same race, at the same time, against live digital twins of your real cars, scored as their own class under one MRA permit. Trial it at the Wakefield 300; scale it to the Sydney 300.

What's in it for MRA

  • New revenue — virtual entry fees + fresh sponsor inventory (class naming, in-sim branding, stream overlays).
  • Bigger audience — reaches the global sim-racing community; streams natively to Twitch / YouTube.
  • A season, not a one-off — a virtual class across all 8 rounds and the three 300 km enduros.
  • A standout story — a genuinely novel hybrid enduro that sets MRA apart and earns media.

Fits your operation

  • Reads your MyLaps timing / GPS — no change to your timing.
  • Relays your one MRA-RMC Zello race-control voice to virtual drivers too.
  • Safety Car → Code 60 for the virtual field, automatically.
  • One operator console beside Race Control.

Safe & fair

  • One-way: virtual cars can never touch a real car or driver.
  • Separate class, transparent scoring; your official MyLaps result is never overridden.
  • Timed finish — virtual cars take the chequer when your real leader completes the distance.
